Oxdelight Journal

A private, voice-first trading journal. Talk through a trade while it happens and the app turns it into a structured entry — direction, entry, stop, target, risk and how you felt — and keeps the original voice note.

What it does

  • Records a short voice note and turns it into a trade entry you review and save.
  • Tracks each trade from idea to closed, with a timeline of updates.
  • Keeps voice notes and chart screenshots in a folder in your own Google Drive.
  • Weekly review, bias forecast and statistics in R.

Who it's for

Oxdelight Journal is a personal tool built and used by its developer. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-ups, ads or in-app purchases.
