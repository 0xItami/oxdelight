Privacy policy

Oxdelight Journal ("the app") is a private trading journal built and used by its developer. This page explains what data the app uses, where it is kept and how to remove it.

Data the app uses from your Google Account

  • Basic profile — your name, email address and profile picture, used only to sign you in.
  • Google Drive — only files the app creates (the drive.file permission). The app saves your voice notes and chart screenshots to an Oxdelight folder in your own Drive and reads them back for playback. It cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive.

Oxdelight Journal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Journal data

Your journal entries (trade levels, notes, transcripts, emotions and weekly forecasts) are stored in a private database hosted by Supabase. Every record is tied to your account, and access is restricted so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. Recordings are kept on your phone until they are safely backed up to your Drive.

Processing by service providers

To turn a voice note into a journal entry, the app's server sends:

  • the audio to a speech-to-text provider (Groq or OpenAI) to produce a transcript;
  • the transcript to Anthropic's Claude API to extract the trade details (direction, levels, risk, emotion).

These providers process the data only to return the result to the app. Audio uploaded for transcription is deleted from the app's temporary storage once it has been processed.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • It does not show ads or track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The app can be locked with a passcode and your device's biometrics. Secret keys for the services above are kept on the server, never in the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete any journal entry from inside the app.
  • Delete the Oxdelight folder in Google Drive to remove all media.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask for your journal database records to be erased via the contact link below.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
